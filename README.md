# Cisza

A small personal meditation page: guided sessions, a sitting timer and a practice journal kept on the device. Non-commercial, personal use.

## Recordings and licences

The recordings in `audio/` are not mine. They are shared here unedited, for non-commercial listening, under the licences their publishers chose. The only change is the file format (converted to mono AAC to keep them small).

- `ucla-*` — [UCLA Mindful](https://www.uclahealth.org/uclamindful/guided-meditations), © The Regents of the University of California, [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).
- `fmp-*` — [The Free Mindfulness Project](https://www.freemindfulness.org/download), [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/), recorded by the teachers named beside each session in the app.
- `ds-<id>` — [Dharma Seed](https://dharmaseed.org/), [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/), each offered by the teacher named beside it in the app. The original talk is at `https://dharmaseed.org/talks/<id>/`.

If you are a rights holder and want a recording removed, open an issue.
